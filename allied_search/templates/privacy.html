Allied Search

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

What Allied Search does

Allied Search forwards your query to several search engines on your behalf, merges their answers, and shows you one list. The engines see this server, not you.

What we collect

Nothing is stored. Queries are held in memory while a search runs and are discarded when the page is sent. There is no database, no search history, no user account, no advertising profile, no analytics and no cookies.

The rate limiter keeps a salted hash of your IP address and a list of timestamps in memory for up to one minute. The salt is regenerated each time the server restarts, so the hashes cannot be linked to you afterwards.

What the search engines see

Each engine receives your query text and this server's IP address, with a generic browser identifier that changes between requests. They do not receive your IP address, cookies or browser details. Each engine applies its own privacy policy to the query it receives.

What the sites in the results see

Result links open the destination site directly and send no referrer. Once you open a site, that site can see your IP address and follows its own privacy policy. Tracking parameters such as utm_source and fbclid are removed from result links.

Your own device and network

Your browser may keep the searches you make in its history. Your network provider can see that you connected to this server. Use a private window or a VPN if that matters to you.

Hosting and logs

Allied Search does not write access logs or query logs. The operator's hosting provider or reverse proxy may keep its own connection logs, outside the control of this software.
